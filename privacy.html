Privacy Policy

Sri Sai Hospital · Effective date: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sri Sai Hospital mobile application ("the App") collects, uses and protects your information when you shop for Ayurvedic products, book appointments or take a queue token with us.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, phone number and email address, provided when you sign in with email, Google or Truecaller.
  • Order & delivery details — delivery address, city, PIN code and the items you order.
  • Appointment & queue details — the doctor, service, date and token number you choose.
  • Device information — a push-notification token so we can send you order and appointment updates.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders, and to send bills and receipts.
  • To manage appointments and the live queue, and notify you when it is your turn.
  • To contact you about your orders or bookings when needed.
  • To keep one secure account per person across your login methods.

3. Sign-in providers

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. If you sign in with Truecaller, we receive your verified phone number and profile name from Truecaller, with your consent shown on their screen. We never see your passwords for these services.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by your own UPI app or by Razorpay, our payment partner. We do not collect or store your card numbers, UPI PINs or banking credentials. We only record whether a payment succeeded.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the services needed to run the App: our secure database and authentication provider (Supabase), our payment partner (Razorpay) and push-notification delivery (Google Firebase). Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

6. Data retention & your rights

Your account data is kept while your account is active. You may ask us to correct or delete your account and personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below, and we will act within a reasonable period.

7. Children

The App is intended for use by adults. Bookings for children should be made by a parent or guardian using their own account.

8. Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by row-level security rules so that only you and authorised hospital staff can see your records.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
